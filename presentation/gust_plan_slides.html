σ and L: the Dryden model

From the mean wind at 20 ft (W20) and height h, for each direction (u along the runway, w vertical):
σw = 0.1 W20,   σu = σw / (0.177 + 0.000823 h)0.4
Lw = h,   Lu = h / (0.177 + 0.000823 h)1.2

T = L / V

The time to fly through one gust of length L at airspeed V: the gust's memory. Recomputed every step.

dW: Brownian motion

Over a step, ΔW = √Δt · Z, with Z ~ N(0, 1). Stepped with Euler–Maruyama / Heun, Δt = 0.01 s:
gn+1 = gn − (gn/T) Δt + σ√(2/T) √Δt Zn

Gusts in the equations of motion

Two new states, ug (along the runway, + headwind) and wg (vertical, + up). They enter only through the air the wing sees: orange.

Ground roll state [x, V], V = ground speed

Va = V + Vwind + ug
α = θ + wg / Va
m V̇ = T − D − μ (mg − L),   ẋ = V
L and D from ½ρVa² S CL(α), CD(α): a gust changes the airspeed and the angle of attack, and with them every force.

Airborne state [x, h, u, w], ground velocity (u, w)

air-relative velocity: va = (u + Vwind + ug,  w − wg)
γa = angle of va,   α = θ − γa
m (u̇, ẇ) = T + L + D + mg,   ẋ = u, ḣ = w
Newton for the ground velocity; lift ⟂ va, drag along −va, both from |va| and α.
Why relative to the ground now: the 11/20 equations (relative to the air) needed the wind's derivative u̇w. A random gust has no derivative: it is built from Brownian kicks. Written for the ground velocity, only the gust's value appears.
In the numerical solution
  • y = [x, h, u, w, ug, wg]: one SDE system, a Heun step every 0.01 s
  • RK4 / RK45 don't apply: their order needs a smooth right-hand side
  • The crew flies the calm-day VEF, VR, reading airspeed with a 1 s lag
One gusty takeoff: grey, the airspeed the wing feels; black, what the crew acts on. Dots: engine failure and rotation.

Then: many gusty takeoffs → a runway with a confidence

Repeat

500 takeoffs on the calm-day schedule, new random gusts each. Sort the runway lengths: the one that covers 95 % (P95), with a confidence interval from order statistics.

Check
  • Generator: gusts have strength σ and memory e−τ/T
  • Time step: expect Euler–Maruyama's gust error ∝ Δt, Heun's ∝ Δt²
  • Runs: sampling error ∝ 1/√N
  • Gusts off: must match the calm-day solver
Final presentation: the extra runway that covers 90 / 95 / 99 % of gusty days, light to severe turbulence, 747-100 and SR-71.
