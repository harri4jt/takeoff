Gust model

Model currently accounts for steady wind, but what about random gusts that can occur?

1Dryden model

Mean wind at 20 ft (W20) and height h → gust strength σ and length L
σw = 0.1 W20,  Lw = h

2Fly through it

The air pattern is frozen; at speed V a gust lasts
T = L / V

3An SDE

Fades over T, kicked by noise, size σ (Ornstein–Uhlenbeck):
dg = −(g/T) dt + σ√(2/T) dW

4Step it

Euler–Maruyama / Heun, Δt = 0.01 s, Zn ~ N(0, 1):
gn+1 = gn − (gn/T)Δt + σ√(2Δt/T) Zn

Then: many gusty takeoffs → a runway with a confidence

Fly

The gust enters the airspeed and the angle of attack:

Vair = V + ug
α ≈ α₀ + wg / V

The crew flies the calm-day V1 and VR, reading airspeed with a 1 s lag.

Repeat

500 takeoffs, new random gusts each. Sort the runway lengths: the one that covers 95 % (P95), with a confidence interval from order statistics.

Check
  • Generator: gusts have strength σ and memory e−τ/T
  • Time step: expect Euler–Maruyama's gust error ∝ Δt, Heun's ∝ Δt²
  • Runs: sampling error ∝ 1/√N
  • Gusts off: must match the calm-day solver
Final presentation: the extra runway that covers 90 / 95 / 99 % of gusty days, light to severe turbulence, 747-100 and SR-71.
