## 5. Simulated ground roll against the closed-form solution

![analytic](../../output/verification/5_analytic_ground_roll.png)

With constant thrust, CL and CD, the ground-roll ODE $m\,dV/dt = A - BV^2$ can be
solved by hand: $x(V) = \frac{m}{2B}\ln\frac{A}{A - BV^2}$ (derivation in
[Physics & equations](equations.md), §7). The full simulator, with all its segments and
events, reproduces it to a relative error of about $10^{-9}$.

## Physics sanity checks (in the summary table)

Each change below moves the BFL the way physics says it should:

* more braking friction, a drag chute, or a headwind → shorter;
* more engine-out drag, a hot day, high elevation, or more weight → longer;
* uphill → longer go, but a shorter stop *after* V1;
* changing the RK4 step from 0.01 s to 0.005 s → BFL unchanged to about $10^{-10}$;
* the touchback guard raises VR when the aircraft would settle back onto the runway;
* the V2 ≥ 1.2 V_S rule is met to within $2\times10^{-4}$ after the k_VR solve.
